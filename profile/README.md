<p align="center"><b>Calm, private, open-source apps you stay in control of.</b></p>

Zimam (زمام, the reins) makes small apps that keep your things on your own device, in plain files, with no accounts and no tracking. Each app lives in its own repository.

| | App | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/zimamlabs/.github/main/brand/rukn-icon.svg" width="48"> | **[Rukn](https://github.com/zimamlabs/rukn)** | A calm reader for PDFs, text and paper books. Covers, lists, reading time, notes and an online lookup for book details. Android first; Linux comes after version 1. |
| <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/zimamlabs/.github/main/brand/falah-icon.svg" width="48"> | **[Falah](https://github.com/zimamlabs/falah)** | Prayer times for where you are, the next prayer and the qibla. Android; notifications are next. |
| <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/zimamlabs/.github/main/brand/dawem-icon.svg" width="48"> | **[Dawem](https://github.com/zimamlabs/dawem)** | Notes, journal, tasks and habits in plain-file vaults. Android and Linux from one codebase. |

Every release carries one file per platform with its own `.sha256`, signed with the project's own key. See each app's README for how to verify a download.

**In the works:** [Rahhala](https://github.com/zimamlabs/rahhala) (trips, places and events, رحّالة), [Rakeb](https://github.com/zimamlabs/rakeb) (ride hailing, راكب), Zimma (money, ذمّة), Himma (health and training, همّة) and Falah (prayer, فلاح).
