<p align="center"><b>Calm, private, open-source apps you stay in control of.</b></p>

Zimam (زمام, the reins) makes small apps that keep your things on your own device, in plain files, with no accounts and no tracking. Each app lives in its own repository.

| | App | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/zimamlabs/.github/main/brand/rukn-icon.svg?v=2" width="48"> | **[Rukn](https://github.com/zimamlabs/rukn)** | A calm reader for PDFs, text and paper books. Covers, lists, reading time, notes and an online lookup for book details. Android first; Linux comes after version 1. |
| <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/zimamlabs/.github/main/brand/zimma-icon.svg?v=2" width="48"> | **[Zimma](https://github.com/zimamlabs/zimma)** | Private money tracking in plain files: accounts, income and spending, monthly totals. Android. |
| <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/zimamlabs/.github/main/brand/falah-icon.svg?v=2" width="48"> | **[Falah](https://github.com/zimamlabs/falah)** | Prayer times for where you are, the next prayer and the qibla. Android, with a notification at each prayer. |
| <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/zimamlabs/.github/main/brand/dawem-icon.svg?v=2" width="48"> | **[Dawem](https://github.com/zimamlabs/dawem)** | Notes, journal, tasks and habits in plain-file vaults. Android and Linux from one codebase. |
| <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/zimamlabs/.github/main/brand/himma-icon.svg?v=2" width="48"> | **[Himma](https://github.com/zimamlabs/himma)** | Health and training in plain files: log meals and calories, track water against a daily goal, and follow a fasting window (16:8 and others). Android. Medicine reminders with Taken and Skip on the notification, a Home page and one visible Zimam folder. Gym and steps come next. |
| <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/zimamlabs/.github/main/brand/rakeb-icon.svg?v=2" width="48"> | **[Rakeb](https://github.com/zimamlabs/rakeb)** | Ride hailing (راكب). 0.1.0 is an offline prototype of the rider flow: fare estimate, simulated ride, history and receipt. No server yet. Android. |
| <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/zimamlabs/.github/main/brand/rahhala-icon.svg?v=2" width="48"> | **[Rahhala](https://github.com/zimamlabs/rahhala)** | Places to go, trips with days, packing and a budget, and events with a guest list and door check-in, in plain files (رحّالة). Android. |

Every release carries one file per platform with its own `.sha256`, signed with the project's own key. See each app's README for how to verify a download.
